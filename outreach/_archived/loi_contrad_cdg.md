**Re: Trellis pilot — request for community endorsement of five sensor host sites in Delta State**

Dear Sir / Madam,

I am writing on behalf of Trellis, a child-centric climate-health intelligence platform being prepared for the UNICEF Venture Fund 2026 Climate Ventures programme. Trellis combines satellite Earth Observation with a 25-node open-hardware sensor network to issue weekly forecasts of childhood asthma, malaria, and pollution exposure at ward level across the Niger Delta. Trellis is open-source, with Nigerian incorporation pending through the Corporate Affairs Commission.

The pilot covers five Delta State LGAs (Warri South, Warri South-West, Burutu, Bomadi, Patani) across 51 wards. After a transparent open-data analysis combining child population, pollution exposure, gas-flare records, and existing health-facility coverage, we have identified five community sites — one per LGA — for the community-managed sensor nodes:

- **Akugbene 2** (Bomadi LGA) — zero existing health facilities
- **Akpakpa** (Warri South-West LGA) — zero existing health facilities
- **Patani 2** (Patani LGA) — zero existing health facilities
- **Tuomo** (Burutu LGA)
- **Pessu** (Warri South LGA)

Three of these wards have no health facility at all. A community-managed sensor in each is the platform's most direct equity statement.

We would like to invite Contrad CDG to be the implementing community partner for these five sites: identifying a custodial host in each ward, coordinating community awareness, and supporting routine sensor maintenance. We propose a virtual or in-person meeting within the next four weeks to discuss the scope of the partnership, with a follow-up site visit to one of the five candidate communities to test community readiness.
