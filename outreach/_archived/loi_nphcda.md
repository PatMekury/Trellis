**Re: Trellis pilot — request for NPHCDA endorsement of ten PHC sensor host sites in Delta State**

Dear Honourable Executive Director,

I am writing on behalf of Trellis, a child-centric climate-health intelligence platform being prepared for submission to the UNICEF Venture Fund 2026 Climate Ventures programme. Trellis combines Sentinel-5P satellite air-quality data, ground-based DePIN sensors, and DHIS2 dashboards to issue 4-week forecasts of childhood malaria, asthma, acute respiratory infection, and pollution exposure at ward (admin-3) resolution. The platform is open-source. Nigerian incorporation is pending through the Corporate Affairs Commission.

The pilot domain is five LGAs in Delta State (Warri South, Warri South-West, Burutu, Bomadi, Patani) covering 51 wards. Ten of the 25 sensor nodes are designated for primary health centres so the platform's forecasts can be cross-validated against operational PHC observation. Using the Trellis composite siting analysis (open-data, reproducible from the project's published scripts) and the GRID3 v2.0 health-facility registry, we have identified the following ten candidate PHC host wards:

- **Bomadi LGA:** Bomadi, Kpakiama
- **Burutu LGA:** Ogulagha, Kiagbodo
- **Patani LGA:** Patani 5
- **Warri South LGA:** Ekurede, Ubeji, Okere
- **Warri South-West LGA:** Ugborodo, Ogbe-Ijoh

Each candidate ward has at least one functional PHC according to the GRID3 v2.0 registry. The exact host PHC in each ward will be identified jointly with NPHCDA's Delta State coordinating office during Phase 1.

The ask is institutional endorsement of the ten candidate wards as PHC host sites, plus a single named NPHCDA point of contact for Phase 1 site finalisation. There is no procurement, no data-sharing precondition, and no cost to NPHCDA at this stage. We would welcome a meeting at NPHCDA's convenience.
