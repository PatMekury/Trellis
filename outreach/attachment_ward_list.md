# Trellis pilot — proposed 25-node sensor allocation

*Attachment to outreach letters of intent.*

> **What this list is, and what it is not.** The wards listed below were selected by a transparent open-data composite score described in the methodology paragraph. **Selection by score is not the same as field-validation.** Each candidate ward still requires (a) confirmation that the proposed channel — PHC, primary school, or community-managed site — is operationally appropriate, and (b) identification of a specific host building and custodian during Phase 1. The ward list is the project's *proposal* to the institutional partner reading this attachment, not a deployment schedule. The host institution per channel is not pre-determined; institutional partners are identified through the outreach this attachment supports.

**Pilot domain:** five Delta State LGAs — Warri South, Warri South-West, Burutu, Bomadi, Patani — covering 51 wards and approximately 700,000 residents (109,000 children under five).

**Network:** 25 open-hardware sensor nodes, allocated 10 PHC-channel + 10 school-channel + 5 community-channel.

**Methodology:** wards selected by composite siting score combining child population (35%), satellite NO₂ exposure (25%), health-facility coverage gap (25%), and gas-flare exposure (15%). Across four alternative weightings, fourteen of the twenty-five placements are unchanged — the stable core. All inputs are open data (GRID3, Sentinel-5P, World Bank GGFR, NASA POWER, WorldPop, CHIRPS); the analysis is reproducible from the project's published scripts.

## What this attachment is for

Each prospective partner is asked to consider, on the basis of this list, whether they could host the rows in the channel they are being approached about. Site finalisation — which specific PHC, school, or community building — is a Phase 1 activity to be done jointly with whichever institutional partner endorses each channel.
