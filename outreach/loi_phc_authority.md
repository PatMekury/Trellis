I am writing on behalf of Trellis, a child-centric climate-health intelligence platform under development for submission to the UNICEF Venture Fund 2026 Climate Ventures programme. Trellis combines Sentinel-5P satellite air-quality data, ground-based DePIN sensors, and DHIS2 dashboards to issue 4-week forecasts of childhood malaria, asthma, acute respiratory infection, and pollution exposure at ward (admin-3) resolution. The platform is open-source. {{trellis_status_paragraph}}

The pilot domain is five LGAs in Delta State (Warri South, Warri South-West, Burutu, Bomadi, Patani) covering 51 wards. Ten of the 25 sensor nodes are designated for primary health centres so the platform's forecasts can be cross-validated against operational PHC observation. Using the project's open-data composite siting analysis and the GRID3 v2.0 health-facility registry, we have identified the following ten candidate PHC host wards:

- **Bomadi LGA:** Bomadi, Kpakiama
- **Burutu LGA:** Ogulagha, Kiagbodo
- **Patani LGA:** Patani 5
- **Warri South LGA:** Ekurede, Ubeji, Okere
- **Warri South-West LGA:** Ugborodo, Ogbe-Ijoh

Each candidate ward has at least one functional PHC according to the GRID3 v2.0 registry. The exact host PHC in each ward will be identified during Phase 1 in collaboration with the institutional partner that endorses the proposal.

The ask is institutional endorsement of the ten candidate wards as PHC host sites, plus a single named point of contact for Phase 1 site finalisation.
