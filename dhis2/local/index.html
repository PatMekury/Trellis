Reading this: month_cos / month_sin capture seasonal cycle. ndvi_annual is vegetation density (proxy for vegetation health and surface water). t2m_mean_lag1/2/3 are temperature 1, 2, 3 months ago. rainfall_mm_lag1/2/3 are lagged rainfall. fac_phc is primary-health-centre count per ward. That NO₂ depends mostly on seasonal cycle + vegetation says the underlying signal is the wet/dry season alternation that pulls pollutants out of the air.
